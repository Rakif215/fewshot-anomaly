Few-shot defect inspection

Show the model a handful of photos of a good part. It then flags defects in new photos and shows where, with no training and no defect examples.

Try it in one click
Or use your own photos
Result

How it works

  1. A frozen DINOv2 vision model turns every region of each photo into a feature vector.
  2. Features from your good photos define what "normal" looks like (a subspace, plus nearest neighbours).
  3. In a new photo, regions that don't fit that picture score high. The top regions give the verdict; the scores give the heatmap.
